How it works

No servers.
No fake votes.

Votes travel invisibly through your guild and group chat. Everyone only sends their own votes, and the game itself sets the sender – so nobody can vote in someone else's name.

Throttled and invisible in chat · votes older than 6 months are cleaned up · votes are not anonymous
